Turning “we should be ethical about this” into things that either exist or don’t

Organisations articulate ethical principles far more than they operationalise them. The distance between the two is called decoupling, and it stays invisible until someone writes both columns side by side. That is all this does, for one rollout.

Keep it generic

No case numbers, no personal data, no internal document text pasted in. Describe mechanisms, not incidents. Everything here should be things you could say out loud in a meeting.
